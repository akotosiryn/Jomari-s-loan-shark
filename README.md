## Favorite Series: *Peaky Blinders*

**Peaky Blinders** is a british crime drama television series set in post-world war 1 birmingham, England. It follows the story of the Shelby crime family. led by the ambitious and cunning **Thomas Shelby**.

### Why i like it
- Intriguing plot and character develpment
- Stylish cinematography and setting
- Powerful performance, especially by *Cillian Murphy*
- Amazing soundtrack that fits the gritty tone
  
 >"By order of the Peaky Blinders."
  if you enjoy historical dramas with a dark and intense vibe, this series is a must-watch.
